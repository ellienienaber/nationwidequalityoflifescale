# Nationwide Quality of Life Scale
This repository contains the Nationwide Quality of Life Scale (NQLS) and associated code/materials from:
1. Nienaber, E.A., Liu, Y., Youngstrom, E.A., & Fristad, M.A. (2026, September). Adolescent-Reported National Norms, Psychometrics, and Clinical Considerations for the Nationwide Quality of Life Scale (NQLS) [Preprint]. medRxiv doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.25.26363826
2. Nienaber, E.A., Liu, Y., Youngstrom, E.A., & Fristad, M.A. (2026, October). Exploring Adolescent-Reported Quality of Life in a National Administration of the Nationwide Quality of Life Scale [New Research Poster Presentation]. Annual Meeting of the American Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry, Atlanta, GA.
